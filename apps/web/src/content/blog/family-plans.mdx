HushOS has family plans. Up to 20 people share one plan's storage, and it covers both their own files and the family folders they share, end-to-end encrypted. Everyone's own files stay private to them. Families are on the web and in the [iPhone and Android apps](/blog/phone-apps).

<StoreLinks className="mt-5" />

## What a family is

A family is a group of HushOS accounts with folders they share. Every member can open the family folders. Their keys are made and sealed on members' own devices, the same way your own files are.

Each member keeps their own files. My files stays private from the rest of the family, exactly as it is private from us. The family folders sit beside it, under the family's name. An account can be in one family at a time.

<FamilyFoldersDemo />

One person organizes the family. They can let others manage it too: invite people, let them in, make family folders and remove someone.

## One plan's storage

A family's storage covers everyone's own files and the family folders together. There is no separate allowance to keep track of: a file in My files and a file in a family folder draw from the same plan.

<FamilyStorageView />

Moving files between My files and a family folder uses no extra storage, because both sides are the same plan. Your device does the move: it downloads each file, locks it again under the keys of where it is going, uploads it, and the original is deleted as the copy lands.

The people who manage the family see how much space each person's own files take, rounded up to the whole gigabyte. They never see the files' names, how many there are, or what is in them. Everyone in the family sees how much the family stores in all, counted the same way, so nobody can work out exactly how much someone else stores.

On a phone, your own files work as before, and the storage the app shows is the family's.

The [pricing page](/pricing) shows the prices in your currency.

Whoever chooses the plan pays for it. Letting in a member past the places paid for adds a place, charged straight away for the rest of the period, and only the person who pays can do that. Fewer places, or a smaller plan, start at the next renewal, and a smaller plan can be chosen only while everything the family stores fits in it.

Polar, which takes the payment, is told it is a HushOS family, who pays and how many places are paid for. It is not told the family's name or who is in it.

If you run HushOS on your own server, families are in the same code and on by default. Without billing there is no shared plan: each family folder has the space you set, and members' own files keep their own.

## Joining

Whoever starts a family names it, then chooses its plan before inviting anyone.

<CreateFamilyScene />

You invite someone by email, and the invitation is open for seven days. They sign in, or make an account, and accept. Then someone who manages the family lets them in, with one click on the family's page. Nobody reads anything out, on either side.

<InviteScene />

When they are let in, the manager's device writes the keys HushOS gives it for the newcomer into the family's signed member list. From then on the newcomer's device holds on to the family it was shown: every later member list has to follow on from it, signed by someone who manages the family, and anything else is noticed and paused, never used.

A newcomer's own files join the family's storage when they are let in, so they have to fit in the family's free space. If they don't, the person letting them in is asked to free up space or move to a bigger plan first.

## Checking each other

Any two members can check each other whenever they like. Each opens the family, chooses Check it's Maya beside the other's name, and the two of you compare twelve words, in person or on a call.

<CheckScene />

**What a check gives you.** The twelve words are worked out from the member list that let the later of you in, and from both your accounts. Matching words mean you both see the same family and each of you has the other's real keys. If they do not match, nothing is saved, and the screen says what to do next. A check is best made with the person who made the family.

**Who is checked first.** Managers can let people in and change the family, so nobody is made a manager, or the organizer, until whoever gives them the role has checked it's them. Someone invited to manage the family joins as a member and gets the role once they are checked.

## When someone leaves

Someone who manages the family chooses Remove from family. The person removed loses the family folders straight away, and the device of whoever removed them replaces the keys of every family folder, which can take a few minutes. Someone who leaves on their own loses the folders straight away too, and their keys are replaced the next time a manager opens the family. Either way, their own files stay theirs, and once the keys are replaced, nothing they kept opens anything new.

Their own files go back to their own storage: Free, unless they choose a plan. If the files take more than that, they can still open, download and delete everything, but can't upload until they make room. Nothing is deleted.

Files keep their authors through all of that. Every file in a family folder carries a signature from whoever wrote it. Replacing keys means sealing every file's keys again, and the easy way would sign each file as the work of whoever replaced the keys. HushOS keeps the writer's original signature instead, and the person replacing the keys signs only that they sealed it again. Info on the file shows both: Written by Sam, Keys replaced by Maya. Your device opens the original and the copy sealed again and compares them itself, so nobody can attach Sam's signature to something Sam did not write.

A file written by someone who has since been removed reads "Written by Sam, who has left Lindqvist family." So you can tell which files came from someone who is no longer in the family.

## When a plan or a family ends

Whoever pays can cancel the plan from the family's page. It stays until the end of the time paid for. When it ends, everyone's own files go back to their own storage, as when someone leaves. Nothing is deleted, and uploading to the family folders starts again when someone chooses a plan.

A family ends when its last member leaves it or deletes their account. 30 days later, its folders and everything in them are deleted from our servers, unless something in them has been reported and is under review. The [privacy policy](/privacy) says how long everything else is kept.

## Encrypted end to end

Family folders are end-to-end encrypted: their keys are made and sealed on members' devices, and our servers store only locked copies. Members can check each other with twelve words, which confirms that nobody, the server included, has slipped in keys of its own. To let the right people in, the server knows the family's name, who is in it and what each person can do. [How HushOS protects your files](/security#sees) lists everything the server knows about a family, and what checking each other rules out.
