## What the apps do

The apps are HushOS, not a viewer for it. They open the same account as the web, with the same keys, and they do what you'd expect a drive to do:

- **Your folders and files**: browse, search, upload, move, rename, copy and trash, with the same colours and tags as on the web.
- **Sharing and links**: share a folder with someone you've added, or make a link, with a password if you like.
- **Families**: the family folders sit beside your own. Managers can invite people, let them in and change roles from the phone.
- **Files kept on the phone**: keep a file or a folder, and it opens without a connection.
- **The phone's own Files app**: HushOS shows up there as a location, so other apps can open and save into it.
- **Save to HushOS**: share a file from another app straight into a folder.
- **Take photo and Scan document**: the + button takes a photo, or [scans paper into one PDF](/blog/scan-documents), edges found and pages straightened.
- **Notifications**: a new sign-in, something shared with you or an invitation to a family, [without Apple or Google learning what happened](/blog/notifications).
- **Make your account**: confirm your email, choose a password and save your recovery kit, all in the app.

Each app looks like it belongs on its phone: system menus, sheets and search, on iPhone and on Android, with HushOS's own folders, marks and colours.

## Private on the phone too

The apps carry no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your files are decrypted only on the phone, into the app's private storage. Signing out removes everything of yours from the phone, and the apps keep your files and lists out of the phone's backups.

Scanning runs on the phone: Apple's own document camera on iPhone, and Google's document scanner on Android, with the usage reports its code would send to Google switched off. The pages are locked before they leave the phone.

## Getting them

- **iPhone**: on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/hushos-encrypted-cloud-drive/id6831238356), for iOS 26 and later. Each [release on GitHub](https://github.com/HushOS/HushOS/releases) also carries an unsigned IPA, with its checksum, for sideloading with AltStore or SideStore.
- **Android**: on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hushos.app), for Android 10 and later. Each [release on GitHub](https://github.com/HushOS/HushOS/releases) also carries the APK, with its checksum, if you'd rather not use Google Play.

Both apps are open source, like the rest of HushOS, and both can sign in to your own HushOS server: the gear on the sign-in screen takes its address.
